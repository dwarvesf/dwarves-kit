## What worked
- Frozen goldens from the Python shim, generated by the orchestrator before the implementer started. Devin could not fit the test to its code, and it found a harness bug instead of hiding it.
- Pre-empting the sibling spec's round-1 findings (latency, log path, truthiness, crash classes) before this spec's first round. Round 1 then found new classes rather than repeats.
- A fresh reviewer that replayed 1,002 real transcripts through both hooks. That surfaced the one realistic-ish divergence (a lone surrogate) that the synthetic corpus could not have predicted.

## What hurt
- `devin -p` with `--sandbox` or `--permission-mode smart` silently rejects file edits in non-interactive mode and exits 0. It cost two runs to learn that only `dangerous` (or the interactive `devin-tab`) edits files headless.
- The harness ran under the C locale until round 1 pointed at the user's UTF-8 locale, which is the environment that actually breaks awk.
- A review fix moved the text two negative controls mutate, so they had to be re-derived and rerun.
